Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lux Christi?

Lux Christi is a Catholic learning platform designed to help users deepen their faith through interactive quizzes, competitions, and community engagement.

How do I register?

Click the "Register" button in the top navigation, fill out your information, and you'll be able to start learning immediately.

Is there a cost to use Lux Christi?

Lux Christi offers a free tier for all users. Premium features are available through our subscription plans.
